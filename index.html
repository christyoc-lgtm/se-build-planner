How to use the Build Planner

Building

  • Pick a block in the left list, then left-click on the build plane or on the face of an existing block to place it.
  • Right-drag orbits the camera, middle-drag (or Shift + right-drag) pans, the wheel zooms.
  • W / S tilt the block's front up / down, A / D turn it left / right, Q / E roll it left / right. X resets. (R / F / V still work too, with Shift to reverse.)
  • 1–4 switch tools: Place, Erase, Pick (eyedropper + block info), Paint.
  • Shift + drag with the Place tool fills a box (great for armour hulls).
  • Symmetry mirrors placement and erasing across X, Y and/or Z planes, just like in-game symmetry mode.
  • The ship's Forward is −Z (red arrow) and Up is +Y (green arrow). Build the cockpit facing the red arrow.
  • C (or ⛓ Conveyor view) shows the conveyor systems: blocks without ports fade out, each connected system gets its own colour, port markers show large (big square) and small (small square) ports, and problem blocks get a red/orange outline. The Conveyor systems panel lists the checks; “Show” jumps to the blocks involved.
  • While placing a block with conveyor ports, its ports are drawn on the ghost: green = will connect, white = open, amber = blocked, red = large/small mismatch. Free ports on nearby blocks show in blue (large) and cyan (small). The left panel lists which way each port faces, and T rotates the block automatically so the most ports connect (thrusters keep their thrust direction).
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo. Ctrl+S saves to this browser. Export/Import moves designs as .json files.

How the numbers are worked out

  • Dry mass is the sum of the block masses (vanilla values; editable through Pick → block info).
  • Cargo = container volume × inventory multiplier × fill % × item density (ore and ice: 0.37 L/kg ≈ 2.7 kg/L). The game shows that full mass, but thrusters and gravity act on cargo ÷ inventory multiplier. So both figures are shown, and the thrust maths uses the physics one.
  • Weight = mass × gravity × 9.81 m/s².
  • Thrust per direction: each thruster pushes the ship one way only, toward the side opposite its nozzle. Its listed force is scaled by its effectiveness at the chosen air density: atmospheric thrusters 0% below 0.3 density up to 100% at 1.0; ion thrusters 100% in vacuum down to 20% at 1.0; prototech down to 30%; hydrogen is always 100%.
  • TWR = effective thrust ÷ weight. Under 1 the ship can't hold itself up with that side facing the sky. A target of 1.5 or more leaves room to climb and to stop a fall.
  • Inertial dampeners use 100% of a thruster's thrust, and Space Engineers applies thrust at the centre of mass, so where a thruster sits doesn't cause torque. The centre of mass is still shown to help balance gyroscopes and landing gear.
  • Hydrogen and oxygen in tanks are treated as weightless, as the game makes them practically massless.
  • Conveyors: two blocks connect when their ports touch face to face. Large↔large and small↔small connect; a large port against a small one does not, so put a Conveyor Converter between them. Hydrogen, oxygen, ore and ingots travel through any connection, but components and large items need an all-large route. The checks: every hydrogen thruster or engine must reach a hydrogen tank, every tank should reach a Connector for refuelling, inventory blocks should share the main cargo system, and component users (assembler, welder, grinder, connector) need large conveyors to the cargo. Port layouts are simplified; if a connection is critical, check the real block in-game.
  • Not modelled: subgrids (rotors, pistons, wheels: add their mass as Extra mass), aerodynamic drag, the speed limit, and thrust override limits.
